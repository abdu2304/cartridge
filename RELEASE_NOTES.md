## Cartridge 0.9.100 · Longshot

### New
- **Longshot, a second way to play Ocarina of Time natively.** Longshot is another native PC version of the Switch 2 edition of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, recompiled from your own copy. Every change it takes in is played on real hardware first, and it keeps a few differences of its own, such as nights as dark blue as on the Switch 2. It's a Cartridge Verified port in PC Ports, from Longshot's own releases. It takes the same copy as Triforce (your .nup, or your romfs and exefs folder), and Cartridge builds it the first time, then starts it straight away.
- **Three ways to play one game.** The Switch 2 Ocarina of Time's page now offers Lichii, Triforce and Longshot. Each port keeps its own build and its own saves, so you can try both.
