## Cartridge 0.9.95 · Captures

### New
- **Captures.** Every screenshot and clip your games made, in one gallery: Start → Quick Menu → Captures. They come from Steam (the screenshots of games Cartridge put in Steam) and from your emulators' own folders, whether they're installed natively, as Flatpaks or by EmuDeck. Each is matched to its game where its name says which. Open one big, step through them with left and right, show it in its folder, or open its game.
- **A game's own captures.** A game with screenshots or clips has Captures in its More.
- **Cartridge only shows them.** Your captures stay where they are: nothing is moved, renamed or deleted.
