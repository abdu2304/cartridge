## Cartridge 0.9.94 · Phone Remote

### New
- **Phone Remote.** Your phone as a remote for Cartridge. Turn it on in Settings → Controls → Phone Remote and scan the code. On your phone you can browse and search your games with their covers, start downloads and follow their progress, show a game on the TV, and move around Cartridge with a D-pad and buttons, as with a controller.
- **Only your home network.** Only phones on the same network can open it, at an address with a secret in it. Save the page to your phone's home screen: the address stays the same until you press New Address. Phone Remote stays on until you turn it off, and turns itself off after 3 hours with no phone.
