## Cartridge 0.9.98 · Switch 2

### New
- **Nintendo Switch 2, played with Lichii.** Get Emulators now has Switch 2: one press installs Lichii from its own releases. Cartridge finds it, starts Switch 2 games in it full screen, puts them in Steam, fills in your keys and firmware from RomM, and looks after its mods, saves and Save Sync the same way it does for Eden. Lichii plays Switch games too, so it's also offered for Switch after Eden and the others.
- **Switch 2 games go in an "ns2" folder.** The Emulation folder Cartridge makes now has `ns2` next to `switch`, and Switch 2 games from RomM download into it.
- **Ocarina of Time on Switch 2: emulated or as a PC port.** The Switch 2 edition of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time can also be played with Triforce, a native PC version recompiled from your own copy. It's a Cartridge Verified port in PC Ports. Give it your .nup or your romfs and exefs folder and Cartridge builds it the first time (10 to 45 minutes, about 60 GB free while it builds), then starts it straight away. The game's page offers both ways to play.

### Changed
- **Emulators with build-numbered releases are kept up to date.** A release named only by its build number (Lichii's "build 341") is now compared properly, so updates are offered.
